### POST


```json
  "password": "Password@123"
}
```

```json
  "message": "User registered successfully"
```

# ✅ Verify Product ↔ Category Relationship

```sql
SELECT
    p.id,
    p.name,
    p.price,
    c.name AS category_name
FROM products p
JOIN categories c
ON p.category_id = c.id;
```

Expected:

```text
1 | Premium Teddy Bear | 599.99 | Premium Soft Toys
```
